# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free community. Be respectful, critique ideas rather than people, assume good intent while addressing harmful impact, and help make technical discussions accessible to newcomers.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [KMerdan GitHub profile](https://github.com/KMerdan). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.
